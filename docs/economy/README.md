# M0 economy: analysis

Numbers come from `docs/economy/report.md` (generated by `node sim/run.js`). Raw data: `day1.csv`, `days.csv`.
Constants: `js/economy.js`. Values marked [?] there wait for the owner's OK.

## The model

As in README §5: stage HP `HP0 * G^(s-1)`, every 10th stage a boss (x `BOSS_MUL`, 30 s timer, missed = back one stage,
stars kept), stars per kill `STAR0 * G_STAR^(s-1)`, tap damage `TAP0 * level`, four heroes with `dps0 * level` doubled at
levels 10, 25, 50, then every 50, level cost `c0 * R^level`. Bedtime pays `floor((maxStage / D_DIV) ^ D_EXP)` Dreams from
stage `D_MIN`. Offline: passive DPS only, capped at 8 h, paid as stars.

Choices the spec left open (proposals):
- `KILLS_PER_STAGE = 5`: five Titans per normal stage, one boss. [?]
- `TITAN_SWAP_S = 0.6`: a Titan takes 0.6 s to fall asleep and the next to arrive. Also a floor on kill speed.
- Dream upgrades multiply: each level of night-lights (taps) or blanket forts (squad) is x2, lullabies (stars) x1.5.
  Linear upgrades (+50% a level) made progress stall for 5 to 6 days at one boss by week 2.
- `OFFLINE_RATE = 0.03`: the squad earns 3% of its normal stars while away.

The simulated child taps at a steady 3, 5 or 8 taps per second, buys the best upgrade it can afford after every Titan,
retries a missed boss when the squad looks strong enough (or after 60 s), plays one session a day.

## Results against the target feel (README §5)

| Target | Result | |
|---|---|---|
| First kill under 5 s | 2.6 s at 5 taps/s, 3.9 s at 3 | met |
| First boss within 3 min | 1.7 min at 5 taps/s, 2.5 min at 3 | met |
| First Bedtime makes sense after 15 to 20 min | progress stalls on day 1 at minute 19 (8 taps/s), 22 (5), 27 (3) | about met |
| 15 min a day: a new world about once a week | world 2 on day 2, world 3 on day 5, world 4 after day 14 | **not met: fast start, then slower** |
| Offline never more than ~30% of a day's progress | at most 13% | met |

Also:
- **Tap speed barely matters**: 3 and 8 taps per second end up within a stage or two after a week. Fair to younger kids.
- **Minutes a day matter less than days**: 10 and 30 minutes a day are 2 stages apart on day 14 (88 vs 90). Playing
  longer does not pay much, which fits "Bedtime means bed".
- **No long walls**: at 15 minutes a day the best stage grows at least every 2 days; bosses at 70, 80, 90 hold a child for 1 to 2 days.

## Findings for the owner

1. **The targets conflict.** "First boss in 3 minutes" and "Bedtime after 15 to 20 minutes" make day 1 reach stage
   23 to 30, almost all of world 1. "A new world every week" with 30-stage worlds would need day 1 to stall around stage 10.
   **M1 has only Pillow Hills (stages 1 to 30), so a child sees the final boss on day 1 or 2.** Options: let stages go
   past 30 in M1 (stronger Titans, same art), or make worlds longer, or accept a fast start.
2. **Offline vs nightly Bedtime.** The spec says Bedtime resets the run, and offline pays from the squad's passive DPS.
   Read literally, a child who goes to Bedtime every evening sleeps with an empty squad and gets 0 offline stars every
   morning. In the sim offline counts the squad as it was when the child said Goodnight; that gives a small morning
   head start (at most 13% of progress). This needs the owner's OK.
3. **Bedtime every evening is the best plan.** Taking Bedtime only when it pays a lot ("smart" in the report) gets stuck at
   stage 70 from day 7; Bedtime every evening reaches 89 by day 14. So the simple rule for a child is also the best one.
4. After about two weeks progress slows down (Dreams grow slower than Titan HP). M2 worlds will need their own boost
   (for example a world bonus) [?]. Not a problem for M1.

## What the sim does not model

Crits, feathers, hero skills, captured toys (they use the built-in classes, so the same numbers), the daily play limit,
a child who forgets to buy upgrades, sessions split across the day.
