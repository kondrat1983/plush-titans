## 1. Pitch

Throw pillows at giant plush Titans until they giggle and fall asleep. Your own toys help you,
and every night at Bedtime they dream and wake up stronger.

## 2. Fit

- **Tap**: the big arena throws a pillow per tap. Crits burst into feathers.
- **Kill**: a Titan "falls asleep" (Zzz), the next arrives after `TITAN_SWAP_S = 0.6` s. 10 per normal stage, 1 boss.
- **Stars**: every sleeping Titan drops stars. Stars buy hero levels and tap power on the Squad screen.
- **Level up**: x1, x10, max. Hero x2 at levels 10, 25, 50, then every 50.
- **Bedtime**: the moon button, always visible. Goodnight resets the run and pays Dreams.
- **Dreams**: spent on three permanent Dream upgrades, so tomorrow goes further and faster.
- The squad of 4 is the toys. A photographed toy is a new look for a hero slot, never new balance.

## 4. Signature mechanic: Bedtime is the prestige and the goodnight

The moon button is on screen all day. When the run stops growing (sim: minute 13 to 20 of a session) or when the parent's
bedtime comes, the child taps it, sees how many Dreams the run is worth, says Goodnight, spends Dreams, and the game shows a
sleeping night screen. Tomorrow starts at stage 1, stronger, with a small morning gift from the squad that "kept catching
stars in its sleep". The sim shows Bedtime every evening is also the best plan (88 vs 70 on day 14), so the simple rule a
6-year-old can follow is the right one.

**Title**
- Logo, a sleeping Titan bobbing (still in calm mode), version label bottom-left.
- Buttons: **Play** (big), **+ Toy**, **Parents** (corner, 90 px or more).
- Play: if locked (bedtime window or daily limit, see 4.3) opens the night screen, else Battle.

**Battle** (layout = approved mock-up v0.2, portrait column; landscape: arena left, controls right)

- Arena: the Titan, tap anywhere in it. Pillow flies to the tap point, damage number floats up, squash on hit.

- Squad row: 4 cards (look, name, "Lv N"). Not yet hired: grey silhouette with the star cost. Tap a card: Squad screen on that hero.
- Bottom: **LEVEL UP** (badge = number of things affordable now) opens Squad; **moon** opens Bedtime.
- Boss missed: a **Try the boss** button (90 px or more, gentle pulse, no pulse in calm mode) sits under the Titan card.
  Stage s-1 keeps looping until it is tapped. Stars from those Titans count.

**Squad**
- Mode toggle at the top: **x1 / x10 / max** (one choice for the whole screen, remembered in the save).
- Card 1: **Tap power**, level, damage per tap, cost, buy button.

  Not hired: "Wake up! 120 stars". Small **Change look** button (opens + Toy for that slot).
- Buy button shows the price for the chosen mode. x10 costs the sum of 10 levels; if short, the button is the flat
  disabled look with the full price. max buys as many as affordable (at least 1, else disabled).
- Back returns to Battle. Combat keeps running while Squad is open (idle game) [?], so time is never lost.
- Fits 4 heroes on one page from iPhone SE to iPad 4:3; uses a scroll if a later version adds more.

**+ Toy** (camera optional; M1 is fully playable without it)

2. Photo: Plush Squad pipeline copied into `js/capture/` (worker loaded only now, one worker per page, WASM single thread).

3. Result: cut-out on a white sticker outline. "Looks like a Bear!" with the 4 class icons to change it [Q4].
   Name field (prefilled with the class name, max 12 letters, kid-safe filter from Plush Squad). **Join the squad**.
4. The toy becomes the look of that class slot. Levels and DPS of the slot do not change.
- Camera refused, error, or 90 s timeout: "We could not see your toy." Buttons **Use the photo as it is** (if a photo
  exists, square crop, no cut-out) and **Pick a plush**.
- Toy Box: saved toys (photo in IndexedDB, metadata in the save), 4 per page with arrows. Max 8 toys in M1 [?].
  Deleting a toy is behind the parent gate; the slot falls back to its built-in look.
- Rotation is blocked while + Toy is open and while the name field has focus.

**Bedtime** (moon button, the bedtime offer, or the daily-limit offer)
- Step A, "Sleepy time?": big number "Dreams you get: 12" (`dreams(maxStage)` + tuck-in bonus if due [Q2]).

  Buttons: **Goodnight** (big) and **Not yet** (only when the child opened it with the moon; absent when the parent's
  bedtime or the limit opened it).

- Step B, "Dream shop": Dream balance, 3 cards (3.6) with level, card text, cost, **Buy**. Buying takes the Dreams at the END
  of the buy animation. **Sleep tight** goes to the night screen. Dreams left over stay for tomorrow.
- Night screen: dark sky, moon, the squad asleep with Zzz, "Goodnight! See you tomorrow." Calm music, no buttons that start a
  battle while locked. If not locked: a small **Back to title** button.

**Parents** (gate first)
- Gate: "Grown-ups only: 7 x 8 = ?" (both factors 6 to 9), number pad, 3 tries then a new question. Wrong answer closes nothing.
- **Bedtime**: Off or a time (step 15 min). **Wake-up time**: default 07:00 [?] (ends the night lock). [Q5]
- **Daily play**: Off, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60 min.
- **Calm mode** (device pref, follows OS reduce-motion on first run). Sound volume is also a device pref, not in the save.
- **Export save**: shows the JSON in a read-only box with **Copy** and **Download**. Toy photos are not included [Q6].
- **Import save**: paste or file, validated, migrated, then "Replace this game?" confirm. Bad file: "That is not a Plush Titans save."
- **Reset progress**: second confirm "Start over? This cannot be undone." Clears the save, keeps device prefs and toy photos [?].
- **What's new** replay. Back.

### 4.2 Offline and the morning gift

- Owner decision: offline at night counts the squad as it was **before** the Bedtime reset. At Goodnight the save stores
  `night = { stage, heroes, dreamLvls, savedAt }` from the run before `bedtime()`. On the next return `offlineStars(night, sec)`
  pays once, then `night` is deleted in the same save. Without a `night` snapshot the current run is used.
- `sec = offlineSeconds(savedAt, now)`: capped at `OFFLINE_CAP_H = 8` h, clock back = 0.
- Paid stars land in the new run. Save at once, so a second launch never pays twice.
- Announcement: if away 5 min or more [?] and stars > 0, a **Welcome back** popup on top of Battle: the squad yawning,
  "While you slept, your squad caught 1.2K stars!", button **Yay!**. Shorter or 0: a small "+N stars" toast, or nothing if 0.
- Shown after the night lock ends, never on the night screen.

### 4.3 Bedtime time, daily limit, never mid-boss

- One check function (`blockedReason()` as in Plush Squad): `bedtime` if now is between the parent's bedtime and wake-up
  time (local, one date helper), `limit` if today's play minutes reached the daily limit, else none.
- Play minutes count only while the page is visible and a battle or menu is open (no idle time, no offline time).
- When the check runs: on every screen open, after each Titan falls asleep, after a boss ends (win or moon full).
  **Never during a boss fight.** A boss that starts 1 s before bedtime is fought to the end.
- `bedtime` or `limit` reached in Battle: the squad pauses, a popup "The Titans are yawning. Time for Bedtime!" (limit:
  "That's all the play for today. Time for Bedtime!") with one button **Bedtime**. After 30 s [?] without a tap it opens
  Bedtime by itself. No countdown is shown.
- After Sleep tight while locked: the night screen stays until wake-up time (bedtime) or the next local day (limit).
  Reopening the app shows the night screen. The popup never says how long is left.
- Bedtime screen while locked: Step A has no **Not yet**. Goodnight is still the child's own tap.

### 4.4 Crits

Mock-up: each tap has a 10% chance to deal x5, "CRIT!" in gold and a feather burst. The sim does not model crits.
Recommendation: crits are **real** (they deal the damage shown) and move into `js/economy.js` as `CRIT_CHANCE = 0.1` and
`CRIT_MUL = 5` [Q1]. Taps only, never the squad. Free random, no money anywhere near it, so it fits play-fair.
Average tap becomes x1.4 (0.9 x 1 + 0.1 x 5). See 5.2.

## 5. Balance

### 5.1 Current values (quoted from `js/economy.js`, unchanged by this doc)

| What | Formula | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Titan HP | `hp(s) = HP0 * G^(s-1)`, boss x `BOSS_MUL` | `HP0 6`, `G 1.35`, `BOSS_MUL 3`. hp(1) 6, hp(10) 268, hp(30) 108K, hp(31) 48.8K |
| Stars per Titan | `STAR0 * G_STAR^(s-1)`, boss x `BOSS_STARS`, x lullaby | `STAR0 1`, `G_STAR 1.3`, `BOSS_STARS 8`. stars(10) 85, stars(30) 16.1K |
| Stage length | `KILLS_PER_STAGE 10`, boss stage 1 | `TITAN_SWAP_S 0.6` |
| Boss timer | `BOSS_TIME 30` s, miss = `bossMissed`: stage - 1, stars kept | |
| Tap | `tap(l) = TAP0 * l * 2^nightlight`; cost `TAP_C0 * TAP_R^l` | `TAP0 1`, `TAP_C0 5`, `TAP_R 1.12` |
| Heroes | `dps0 * l * 2^milestones(l)`; cost `c0 * R^l` | `R 1.08`; dps0/c0 in 3.5; x2 at 10, 25, 50, then every 50 |
| Dreams | `floor((maxStage / D_DIV)^D_EXP)` from `D_MIN` | `D_MIN 20`, `D_DIV 5`, `D_EXP 1.5`. Stage 20: 8, 27: 12, 30: 14 |
| Dream upgrades | x(1 + step) per level; cost `round(cost0 * costR^lvl) + lvl` | 3.6 |
| Offline | passive only, last normal stage, `* OFFLINE_RATE` | `OFFLINE_CAP_H 8`, `OFFLINE_RATE 0.03` [?] |

run stalls at minute 21 on day 1; day 14 at 15 min a day: stage 88; offline at most 9% of progress at 15 min a day.

3. Tuck-in bonus over 14 days: best stage by day vs today's table.
4. Night snapshot rule: already modelled ("offline counted from the squad BEFORE the reset"), keep it as the default.
5. A child who only buys x1, and one who only taps max: no stall longer than one day in 14 days.

## 10. Mismatches between docs and code

- Crits are in the mock-up (`Math.random() < .1`, x5) but not in `js/economy.js` or the sim (`docs/economy/README.md`, "does not model").
- Mock-up names Hoot in the boss line for every boss stage; M1 has 3 bosses (3.3).
- Mock-up tap and DPS values are fake ("mock damage"); the game uses `tap()` and `squadDps()`.
- Mock-up text: world label 13 css px, hero level 12, HP numbers 14; mute button 48 css px. Check they reach 24 / 90 game px
  in the Phaser scene [?].
- `tapCost` and `heroCost` return fractions (`tapCost(1) = 5.6`). The game needs one rounding rule (recommend `Math.ceil`,
